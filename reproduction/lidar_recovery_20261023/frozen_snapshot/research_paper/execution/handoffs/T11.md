## Pilot variability checks and commands

Three additional seed pairs (11–13) were generated and replayed sequentially.
Each bag pair has 400 scans and 8,021 byte-identical IMU messages. Each replay
accounts for all scans and yields 397 valid diagnostic groups, 397 poses,
754/794 valid evaluation rows and zero reference gaps. Both health audits and
reference-hash checks pass.

Example exact commands for one pair (repeat with seeds 12 and 13 and their
matching `control` input/output names):

```bash
ROS_ENV/bin/python research_paper/experiments/src/write_simulation_bag.py \
  --output research_paper/experiments/generated/simulation_bootstrap/sim-recovery-dev11-v3 \
  --duration 40 --seed 11
ROS_ENV/bin/python research_paper/experiments/src/write_simulation_bag.py \
  --output research_paper/experiments/generated/simulation_bootstrap/sim-recovery-control11-v3 \
  --duration 40 --seed 11 --control
bash research_paper/experiments/run_simulation_smoke.sh \
  ROS_ENV PATCHED_WORKSPACE \
  research_paper/experiments/generated/simulation_bootstrap/sim-recovery-dev11-v3/sensors.bag \
  research_paper/experiments/generated/runs/SIM40_v3_seed11_corridor
bash research_paper/experiments/run_simulation_smoke.sh \
  ROS_ENV PATCHED_WORKSPACE \
  research_paper/experiments/generated/simulation_bootstrap/sim-recovery-control11-v3/sensors.bag \
  research_paper/experiments/generated/runs/SIM40_v3_seed11_control
```

The exact input/output paths and hashes are in the seed-sensitivity manifest.
