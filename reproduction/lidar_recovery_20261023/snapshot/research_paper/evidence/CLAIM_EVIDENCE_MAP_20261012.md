## Classification rule

**Predeclared estimation** means the endpoint and calculation were fixed before final estimator outcomes; it does not mean a directional hypothesis, significance test or novelty claim was prespecified. `CONFIRMATORY_COMPLETE` is the workflow's complete-data acceptance status, not proof of every interpretation. **Exploratory interpretation** means the narrative, grouping emphasis or explanation was chosen after seeing results, even when its component numbers came from preplanned summaries. **Unsupported** means the claim exceeds the available design, comparison or uncertainty.

## Exposure and next-study boundary

The 48 final geometries, labels, scores and outcomes are now exposed. New width hypotheses, chosen examples, altered thresholds, extra comparator choices and mechanism narratives cannot be backdated as predeclared. The old failed screen already informed the geometry amendment; disclose that separately from the absence of final outcome tuning. No formal p-value, new pooled confidence interval or multiple-comparison procedure is introduced here.

If future work tests an exploratory claim, define the hypothesis, nuisance controls, endpoints, comparison and sample-size rationale before generating fresh evaluation IDs. Use development-only checks, retain the current cohort as discovery evidence, and freeze the new protocol. Do not call existing geometry strata a new untouched replication or expand the current sample based on favorable outcomes.
