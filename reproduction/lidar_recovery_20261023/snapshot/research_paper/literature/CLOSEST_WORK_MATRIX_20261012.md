## Search and access record

Searches combined the named papers with LiDAR degeneracy, recovery, false-healthy, held-out geometry and threshold transfer. Primary sources above were used for technical conclusions; third-party summaries/search rankings were not evidence. X-ICP/SuperLoc/ICA/DCReg/OCE were inspected in full-text HTML at the linked versions; LOFF publisher search text exposed its detector and experiment sections when direct opening initially failed. ALIVE-LIO, AdaLIO and GEODE were inspected in primary full-text HTML. Sections cited define the comparison boundary.

The earlier [DCReg supplement audit](RECOVERY_OVERLAP_UPDATE.md) and [ICA audit](INFORMED_CONSTRAINED_ALIGNED.md) remain relevant. This refresh did not independently re-open the DCReg supplementary PDF or inspect ICA supplementary videos; prior supplement observations are not promoted into new verified assertions. Exact public implementation parity and additional unpublished/supplementary evaluations remain outside this comparison.

This is a targeted closest-work refresh, not a systematic review with exhaustive databases, complete citation snowballing or a proof of nonexistence. Search hits about LiDAR resolution reconstruction, forest recovery and navigation-policy recovery address different tasks and were excluded. Further literature review and scientific feedback can overturn the candidate gap.

## Assessment

OCE is the strongest direct overlap; DCReg and ICA further constrain the argument. No “first recovery assessment,” “first false-alarm study,” “first multi-scene benchmark” or generic geometry-dependence claim is justified. The exact combination of fixed-policy transfer across this screened design and two measurement-stage replications was not identified in the selected sources. Its scientific value must come from useful, reproducible findings, not combining existing protocol features.
