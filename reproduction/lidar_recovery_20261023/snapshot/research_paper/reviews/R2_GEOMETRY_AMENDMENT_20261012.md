## Methodological assessment

1. **Bounded spacing addresses the failure mechanism.** Four baffles become `max(4, ceil(length_m / 2 m) - 1)` with positions `length_m * i / (count + 1)`. Thus all longitudinal gaps, including the ends, are at most 2 m. Height, wall-side extent and the open central route remain unchanged. Explicit source comparison shows corridor/room surface lists unchanged for equal dimensions. This does not prove unchanged visibility from controls: added baffles can occlude room returns or influence estimator initialization, so native development validation is required.
2. **Eligibility remains geometry-only and fixed.** Corridor axial-normal fraction <=0.10, control >=0.15 and corridor post-exit (+5 s) >=0.20 use the original formal route and coverage algorithm. Axial-normal coverage is a specified eligibility proxy, not proof of estimator observability or accurate motion. The repair does not increase short-wide eligibility; larger pools merely improve the chance of finding the required eligible layouts.
