## Decisions implemented

- Transform notation is `T_A_B`, mapping coordinates from `B` into `A`; pose rows are `T_parent_body`.
- Frames are right-handed; metric units are SI; quaternions are Hamilton `qx,qy,qz,qw`.
- Canonical time is integer nanoseconds. The development origin is the first LiDAR header timestamp.
- The evaluator performs bracketed linear/SLERP reference interpolation only inside a declared coverage segment, with a provisional maximum 0.20 s bracket. It never extrapolates or bridges outages.
- Pose windows remain within one estimator/reference segment and use a provisional ±0.05 s endpoint tolerance.
- Relative error is `inverse(Delta_reference) * Delta_estimate` over provisional 1 s primary and 3 s sensitivity windows.
- Accumulated error uses a single pre-entry pose anchor at `entry_start - 5 s`, fixed `SE(3)` scale 1 alignment, with no later alignment.
- Reset, crash, missing output, unknown body frames, and unavailable truth are explicit states.
- Recovery tolerances, sustained duration, indicator operating points, and final horizons remain assigned to T11; T05 does not fabricate them.
- Reference truth and transition annotations are isolated from backend/indicator APIs and enter only the offline evaluator.
