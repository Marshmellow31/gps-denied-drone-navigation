## Recommendation in plain language

The best *candidate to inspect next* is Hilti-Oxford 2022 **Exp18 Corridor Lower Gallery 2**. Unlike the GEODE recording we tried, its publishers state that the reference path is for the IMU frame, and they publish a LiDAR-to-IMU calibration. Exp18 has a dense position-and-orientation reference file. This solves the *documentation* problem, but not the entire research problem: we have not verified the bag contents, found an independent weak-to-rich transition timestamp, or shown that FAST-LIO2 works on the Hesai data. Its reference poses were computed by matching the same mobile LiDAR's scans to a separately surveyed static map, so they are not a completely independent physical tracking measurement, especially in weak geometry. Keep this as a **provisional development candidate**, not verified truth for final claims.

## What was verified for the Hilti candidate

- The [official challenge page](https://hilti-challenge.com/dataset-2022) identifies Exp18 as an additional hard corridor/lower-gallery sequence with 6-DoF reference, distinguishes it from Exp07's 3-DoF-only corridor, says all ground truth is in the IMU frame, and documents synchronized Hesai point clouds with per-point timestamps. The [publisher's dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hilti-Research/hilti-slam-challenge-2022) names the three dense-reference sequences and states the non-commercial CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 terms.
- The [published calibration file](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hilti-Research/hilti-slam-challenge-2022/blob/main/calibration/calibration_files/lidar_calibration.yaml) declares `imu` at identity under `base_link`, and `PandarXT-32` under `imu` with quaternion `[0.7071068,-0.7071068,0,0]` (xyzw) and translation `[-0.001,-0.00855,0.055]` m. This provides a direction-explicit frame tree for a proposed IMU-body comparison; the actual bag frames still need inspection.
- The [Exp18 dense reference](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hilti-Research/hilti-slam-challenge-2022/blob/main/ground_truth/exp18_corridor_lower_gallery_2_imu.txt) was read as a small text stream, not used for backend selection or tuning. It has **789** eight-field rows over **86.596 s**, non-identity changing quaternions, nominal roughly 10 Hz cadence, and a largest adjacent time gap of about **1.300 s**. Gaps must remain unavailable; the full coverage pattern at a future scene event is unknown. The publisher also offers dense files for Exp14 (689 rows, 73.793 s, max gap 0.606 s) and Exp16 (2,001 rows, 200.091 s, max gap 0.200 s).
- Publisher file metadata reports Exp18 bag **8,657,654,860 bytes**, Exp14 **6,260,771,085 bytes**, and Exp16 **15,523,312,816 bytes**. On this host only about **2.5 GiB** was free at inspection. The existing 1.8 GB GEODE subset and 4.3 GB experiment package cache were not removed. A full Exp18 download is unsafe without a validated storage/streaming plan; no cleanup or download was performed.
- [Hilti-Oxford's paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.09825), section on dense ground truth for development, describes offline registration of undistorted mobile scans to a surveyed prior map, approximately 1–2 cm accuracy for development, and advises surveyed sparse points for sub-centimetre evaluation. The dense reference's errors may correlate with LiDAR geometry; this is a scientific limitation, not a reason to silently call it independent truth.
- The currently pinned FAST-LIO2 `src/preprocess.cpp` handles Ouster, Velodyne and simulation `PointCloud2` formats, but has no Hesai-specific handler. A narrowly tested field/time adapter or an explicitly revised backend choice would be needed. We have **not** established which adapter is valid from the actual Exp18 bag.

## Decision gate before downloading or replaying

1. Establish safe space for at least the 8.66 GB source bag and any temporary/subset files, or prove a lossless LiDAR+IMU streaming extraction plan without filling the 2.5 GiB remaining volume. Do not delete retained GEODE inputs or run records merely to make room.
2. Inspect Exp18 source topics, point fields/time convention, IMU units/clocks, exact calibration direction and reference coverage. Preserve all needed pre-transition initialization data. Exclude cameras from estimator input, but do not assume they can be dropped safely without a verified subset.
3. Mark the corridor-to-gallery boundary using scene evidence only, before seeing indicator values or error peaks. Confirm the *post-transition* reference interval supports the planned 1 s and 3 s windows and fixed pre-event alignment.
4. Test an adapter on actual cloud timestamps and run one short FAST-LIO2 replay. If motion tracking fails again, retain the run and bring backend/data choice to the R1 review instead of tuning against a final test.
5. R1 must decide whether map-registration reference dependence is acceptable for **development only** and whether final conclusions need a truly independent external 6-DoF reference or a narrower claim. No current candidate silently passes that scientific gate.
