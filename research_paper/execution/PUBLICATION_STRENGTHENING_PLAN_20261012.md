# Plan to strengthen the publication case

Saved 12 October 2026 for future reference. This is a proposed plan, not a completed scientific review, accepted protocol amendment or authorization to run new experiments. The controlled evaluation is complete; publication novelty and the manuscript remain unfinished.

Strengthen the scientific argument and resolve specific weaknesses rather than adding runs without a clear purpose.

## 1. Make the contribution precise

Frame the candidate paper around the observed limitation: fixed geometric health rules can trade premature reassurance against missed recovery declarations when transferred to new geometries. Explain what this adds beyond existing work, particularly [OCE's usability and recovery evaluation](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43684-026-00144-1).

Use the [candidate contribution](../literature/CONTRIBUTION_ASSESSMENT_20261012.md), [closest-work matrix](../literature/CLOSEST_WORK_MATRIX_20261012.md) and [claim-to-evidence map](../evidence/CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MAP_20261012.md). Do not claim a new detector, a first recovery study or superiority to systems not evaluated here.

Deliverable: a concise contribution statement explaining what readers learn beyond the closest papers.

## 2. Address endpoint ambiguity

Present **health is active** and **a new recovery declaration occurs** separately. The [failure investigation](../evidence/final_failure_analysis_20261012/FAILURE_ANALYSIS_REPORT.md) found that some missed declarations already had active health. Explain which endpoint matters for the intended application and why.

Preserve the frozen original results and definitions. Additional analyses chosen after inspecting these exposed data are exploratory; do not backdate them as predeclared or replace the original headline with a more favorable endpoint.

Deliverable: an explicit operational interpretation of both endpoints, with existing evidence and limitations.

## 3. Strengthen comparator fairness

Assess whether a closer comparator can be implemented faithfully in the evaluated backends. Keep the current DCReg detector adaptation distinct from full DCReg. An OCE adaptation requires a compatibility and fidelity assessment before comparison; its optimizer quantities cannot be assumed equivalent to the existing LIO diagnostics.

Before adding a comparator, document its equations, input stage, timing, calibration, source identity and adaptation limits. Validate on development data and obtain the required dated protocol review. Without a fair shared evaluation, avoid superiority claims.

Deliverable: a comparator feasibility decision, followed by an implementation/validation plan only if justified.

## 4. Confirm one important explanation on fresh data

The observed width pattern is a candidate explanation, not an established mechanism. First identify plausible causes from the existing recordings, then design a focused experiment controlling other factors.

Freeze the hypothesis, factor changes, nuisance controls, settings, sample-size rationale, endpoints and analysis before generating fresh evaluation cases. Retain the current cohort as exposed discovery evidence. Do not retune on it, relabel it as untouched confirmation or expand its sample based on favorable outcomes.

Deliverable: a reviewed, frozen confirmation protocol addressing a specific scientific objection, followed by honest confirmation or failure to replicate.

## 5. Broaden validation only as far as the claim requires

For a narrowly scoped simulation paper, explain the simulator assumptions and test robustness conditions relevant to the claim through a separately approved design. For real-world claims, obtain recordings with suitable independent reference and enough recovery transitions. The current Hilti development pilot cannot supply sustained independent real-world recovery evidence.

Keep conclusions conditional on eligible simulated straight-corridor geometries until broader evidence exists. No flight-safety claim follows from this work.

Deliverable: a justified validation scope and clearly stated generalization limits.

## 6. Write the manuscript and obtain scientific criticism

Complete the manuscript using audited results, failure analysis and documented exposure limits. Ask a professor or domain expert whether the contribution is distinct and useful, whether the endpoint interpretation is appropriate, and whether any proposed additional experiment resolves a real objection.

Implementation and accounting acceptance are not publication-novelty acceptance. Do not present automated review as journal peer review.

Deliverable: a complete draft and actionable scientific feedback.

## Recommended order and decision rule

1. Draft the paper around the bounded empirical finding.
2. Resolve the interpretation of active health versus a new recovery declaration.
3. Assess comparator feasibility and fairness.
4. Freeze one focused confirmation study if the claim or scientific feedback requires it.

If the literature comparison shows no sufficiently distinct contribution, narrow or change the question. Novelty cannot be established through wording alone, and more experiments alone do not resolve an unclear contribution.
