## Inspection result

- `/imu/data`: monotonic 100 Hz-class stream, `imu_link`, unit quaternions; all covariance arrays are zero and therefore treated as unavailable rather than zero uncertainty.
- `/velodyne_points`: monotonic 10 Hz-class stream, `velodyne`, 16 rings, little-endian `x/y/z/intensity/ring/time` schema, 82,660,064 total points.
- Point-time sign/reference remains a T06 calibration and convention audit item.
- The published trajectory is a six-degree-of-freedom RTK/INS-derived reference, but the release does not document datum, axis/body convention, fix status, covariance, or achieved trajectory accuracy. The CGI610 1 cm figure is an instrument specification, not sequence-level truth accuracy.
- Reference samples are non-monotonic in file order and contain two long outages. Sorting is permitted only within independently declared continuous segments; interpolation across the tunnel outage is prohibited.
- The released alpha configuration contains a duplicate `gyro_std` key and does not prove transform direction from key names. It must not be parsed blindly.
